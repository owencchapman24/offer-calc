# Audit, November 2026

`main` at `09c2ea6` (2026-11-10). A read-only audit: six auditors covered tax, pay streams, comparisons and leaving, app state,
figures and outputs, and tooling. Nothing in the code, the docs or the settings was changed. Line references are to that commit.
Each finding has a rank:
- **fix before launch**;
- **should fix**;
- **nice to have**.

✔ means the finding was re-checked by hand after the auditors reported it: by reading the code, re-running a script or a web
search. "Re-checked 2026-11-21" means it was checked again on `5f5c39c` and is still open.

The report was saved here on 2026-11-21, with the findings as reported. The numbers (N1-N49) and the Status column were added
then. When a pull request fixes a finding, update its Status.

## Summary

**Verdict:** the core holds up. The problems are concentrated in code added since October: 4h (commission), 4i (the ESPP), 4j
(other benefits) and #79's split of the worker's chunks. Some data is also behind.

**What's in good shape:**
- **The engine's arithmetic:** across 59 projections, the after-rent check holds to the cent, the years add up to the window, and
  there's no NaN. They covered Feb 29 and month-end starts, leave dates at the edge of an anniversary, and stays of 1 to 10 years.
- **Federal 2026 law** matches the IRS's 2026 figures. A scan from $10k to $2M of income jumps only where the law does.
- **Security is clean:**
  - a tight CSP and no `innerHTML`;
  - everything escaped in Copy as document;
  - hostile names guarded in every CSV;
  - `__proto__` in an import is harmless.
- **CI and history:** CI is pinned and strict, and there are no secrets in the history.

**Since (2026-11-22):** ten of the twelve fix-before-launch findings are fixed: N1-N7 and N10, in #83-#93, N8 in #97 and N9 in
#98. N5 had a leftover, fixed in #94. The two left, N11 and N12, are the user's decisions. Of the should-fix items, N14 is fixed
in #95, and N26, N15, N16, N17 and N18 in #99-#103. #82, merged first, committed the tax baselines and checks the grid in CI
(October's T1), so the 2027 refresh (N1) was the first change it checked.

**What's open, by area:**
- **Engine:** N13 (the ESPP's cycle). N15 (breakeven's throw), N16 (L-4 past year 5) and N17 (the clawback's months) are fixed.
- **Tax data:** N27 (California's amounts, in December). N26 (the Massachusetts surtax) is fixed.
- **Commission's UI:** N19 and N20. N18 (its problems' names) is fixed.
- **The app's robustness:** N21-N24.
- **CI hygiene:** N34-N36.
- **The user's decisions:** N11 (the owner's offer in a public doc) and N12 (Vercel waiting for CI).

## Fix before launch

### N1. The IRS's 2027 amounts are out and not in the engine
✔ Rev. Proc. 2026-35 came out on 2026-11-05, but `federal2027.ts:17` still said they were unpublished.
- The 2027 standard deduction is $16,600 (single) and $33,200 (joint); the engine projected $16,502.50.
- At $100k single in Texas, 2027's tax was $12,949 projected, against about $12,890 under the published law.
- It's the first change #82's baseline would check. The 402(g) notice wasn't out yet.

**Fixed:** #83 (the brackets, head of household's among them, the standard deduction, and the 127 and 132(f) limits; the 18
bracket-floor taxes pinned in `test/law.test.ts`) and #84 (the 402(g) limit).

### N2. Tax-free other benefits are left out of L-4's "total rewards kept"
✔ Two auditors found this independently.
- The list at `leave.ts:74` omits `otherBenefit`, though `project.ts:370` counts it.
- It affects L-4, A-4 and the value of staying to the next cliff, and its Show the work doesn't add up.

**Fixed:** #87. It also made one list of the tax-free kinds, and the stronger property test found a $1 rounding error in the same
derivation, fixed there too.

### N3. Pay due after the projection's end is dropped when the last day is late
✔ October's C6 is still open, and commission now has the same bug (`commission.ts:82`, `streams.ts:147`, `project.ts:68`).
- L-4's axis runs 5 years, so the default 4-year stay is exposed.
- Example: a last day of 2031-06-30 drops the 2032 bonus, with no warning.

**Fixed:** #92. A payout owed after the last day is followed to its pay date, for bonuses and commission.

### N4. L-4's "the cliff itself" counts payouts you'd get anyway
✔ `cliffs.ts:133-136` adds every bonus or commission paid on the cliff's date, including ones you'd keep by leaving the day before.
- A script: $9,218 for "the cliff itself", against $5,119 for the whole value of staying.

**Fixed:** #88. The cliff counts only the payments it controls.

### N5. A-6 and A-7 show different tax figures for the same year
✔ Re-run: sample A, 2028, federal tax is 32,947 in A-6 and 32,949 in A-7.
- It breaks CLAUDE.md's "one figure" rule (section 6). Each table rounds its own way (`partsModel.ts:283`, `workpaper.ts:520`),
  and no test ties them.

**Fixed:** #89 (A-6 takes A-7's rounding) and #94 (the withholding derivation's federal figure, $1-2 off, left over from #89).

### N6. With one offer, an engine error leaves stale figures looking current
✔ Read: `useEngine.ts:111` keeps the old breakdowns, and nothing passes the error to A-1 (`App.tsx:284`).
- It's more likely since #79: a tab left open across a deploy can fail to load the commission or ESPP module.
- October's C24 (no recovery when a chunk fails to load) is wider now too.

**Fixed:** #91. A-1 says the engine couldn't compute, and why; old figures are marked "Last valid results" with any number of
offers; a missing chunk says to reload. The rest of C24 is open.

### N7. A rent of $0 shows "plus rent $0 a month -0"
✔ October's C16, now in the bridge, A-7 and the copied document (`text.ts:8,10`, `byYearModel.ts:271`).

**Fixed:** #90. The shared formatters round before taking the sign. Its property check also found "Portland $-135,490" in a label,
fixed there too.

### N8. Bear, base and bull ranges still aren't checked for order
October's C7. Commission's new range isn't checked either.

**Fixed:** #97. Each range is checked in order once its cases pass their own checks: bear <= base <= bull, and weekly hours
bear >= base >= bull (the bear case works the most, the user's choice). A case on the wrong side is a problem at its field
(`caseOrder`), and the lead names it with its field and case, where it said "Input".

### N9. Bad imports give NaN or a throw
✔ October's C25 is only partly fixed.
- Four BEA areas have zero data in `rpp.engine.json`: 10999, 11999, 34999 and 44999 (the state nonmetropolitan portions of
  Delaware, the District of Columbia, New Jersey and Rhode Island). Re-checked 2026-11-21: still zero.
- An area named `constructor`, and values like 1e308, pass validation.

**Fixed:** #98. Every dollar amount is at most $100,000,000, a rate of change 100% a year, a list 120 rows and an import 1 MB;
areas and states are looked up by own key, and an area needs prices. About you's wages are bounded where they're typed and
imported. Whole numbers of months, years and days are N17's, fixed in #102.

### N10. A flat local tax can't be entered, though the docs say it can
✔ CLAUDE.md section 10, M-10 and the state notes say it can, but `model.ts:398` builds only NYC or Philadelphia.
- So Baltimore County's tax (about $4,500 at $150k), and Ohio's and Pennsylvania's local taxes, can't be counted at all.

**Fixed:** #93: a flat local income tax field, in detailed mode under Where you'd live.

### N11. The owner's real offer is in a public doc
✔ Row 3 of `docs/user-testing.md` describes the owner's own offer in detail, and the README names the owner. The owner decides;
it goes with the repository's visibility (CLAUDE.md section 3, the pre-launch checklist's item 1).

### N12. Vercel still deploys `main` before CI passes
October's S1. The `main` ruleset holds pull requests to the CI checks, but Vercel promotes each push to `main` without waiting for
them.

## Should fix

- **N13.** Offerings of 9, 18, 24 or 27 months join a cycle late (`espp.ts:47`), contrary to the help text. Re-checked 2026-11-21:
  `espp.ts` still anchors offerings from the offering day a year before you join.
- **N14.** Pay received after the last day goes into the ESPP and is then "refunded" (`espp.ts:62`). **Fixed:** #95.

**Leaving early and breakeven**
- **N15.** Breakeven throws when the lever can't change the result, and that blanks all of L-3 (`breakeven.ts:84`). **Fixed:** #100,
  on 2026-11-22: two outcomes, "notInWindow" and "outOfReach", instead of the throw.
- **N16.** For stays over 5 years, L-4's cursor finds no cliff after year 5. **Fixed:** #101, on 2026-11-22. Its fix found more:
  for those stays, L-1 and each offer's A-3 stopped at year 5, and the stay's reconciliation counted the later years as past the
  stay (−$357,694 for sample A over 7 years). The totals were right. Fixed in the same pull request.
- **N17.** Clawback months have no cap, and `cliffs()` slows with the square of the months: 10,000 months takes 68 s and stalls
  the worker. **Fixed:** #102, on 2026-11-22: a number of months, years or days after the start is at most 10 years' worth, the
  longest stay (120 months, 10 years, 3,653 days). At 120 months, `cliffs()` takes 24 ms.

- **N19.** The draw's Show the work shows a raw input path and "$3".
